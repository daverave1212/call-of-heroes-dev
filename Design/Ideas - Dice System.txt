
---------- Other Game Check Systems ---------
- d20 + bonus (e.g. D&D)
- Xd6 + bonus (e.g. QuestGuard, Powered by the Apocalypse)
- d100 roll below (e.g. Call of Cthulhu)
- d100 roll above (e.g. MERP)
- Forged in the Dark - Xd6, highest number (e.g. Blades in the Dark)
- Dice Pools - Xd6 6=success (e.g. Vaesen, Drakonym, Burning Wheel, Eldritch Horror)
- Poker dice - Xd10 and find pairs and matches; singles are fails (e.g. Nemesis, Reigh)
- Blackjack - highest value below 21 (e.g. Pendragon)
- Red/Green dice - Gd6 - Rd6; positive = success (e.g. Genesys, Star Wars)










  New Check System:
Players have 4 Stats (Might, Instinct, Focus, Presence) ranging:  -1 to +2 (Level 1) up to +4 (Level 5)
Players have Skills (e.g. Jumping, Smelling) ranging:             -1 to +2 (Level 1) up to +4 (Level 5)

The DCs for each table are chosen in such a way that it has the best curve I could obtain.

----------- III. ROLL + SCALING BONUS -----------
This one needs explaining, because the curve is similar to II. ROLL + STAT or SKILL, but the way you assign stats and skills is different:
- You start with 4 Stat Points, at 0/0/0/0 (you can go negative to gain more stat points)
- Each +1 in a Stat costs 1 Stat Point + 1 for each point already in that Stat (e.g. if you have 1 Might, to get to 2 Might you need to invest 2 Stat Points).
- Skill points work exactly the same, but it's easier to reach a +3 since you get more Skill Points.

----------- III. DICE POOL -----------
The idea of dice pools is that you roll that many D6's.
Each 5 and 6 counts as a success.
You need an amount of successes to pass the check.

J. ADDITIVE SKILLS
In this variant, each Skill only counts as +1, but you can apply multiple skills when doing a Check.
No DC chart even needed here.
    Ideas:
o Players can opt to try to generate 1 Plot Points by adding at 1 negative Skill (per PP)
o Limit each Skill to 1 word
    Pros:
+ Super simple and easy to understand, even simpler than what I currently have
+ Gets rid of stacking item and skill bonuses
+ 2d6 no longer needed for generating Plot Points, so I have more freedom with the dice rolls to make the curve better.
+ If I impose the rule of "no one Skill you have can be fully included into another Skill", then the players can also choose how broad their skills are. E.g. legwork, perception, but then they can't also get Jumping, or Running, because they're both basically covereed by Legwork. Or they could go much more specialized like 
+ Players must make more interesting choices when creating characters and choosing their skills
+ Character creation can incorporate Flaws, which are the opposite of skills.
    Cons:
- Might actually achieve the opposite of what I want, because players might get a ton of Skill to stack them up.
- Can't know how good it is without playtesting
- 2d6 no longer needed (I really liked you buddy...)








----------- IV. THINKING THE OTHER WAY AROUND -----------
Here are the bonuses on dice that I find acceptable ranges (e.g. average - max):
2d6:            +3 - +5         |   3-5 have good percentages on DC's, with +6 being an outlier with acceptable percentages
3d6 (A):        +3 - +5         |   12, 15, 18 as DC's for easy, medium, hard.
3d6 (A):        +4 - +6         |   14, 16, 18, 20 as DC's for easy, medium, hard, very hard, though the numbers are quite awkward
Dice Pool (A):  +4 - +6         |   1, 2, 3 (successes on 5,6) as DC's for easy, medium hard. However, +7 as an outlier vastly overpowers +6, while +3 is quite a lot below the curve. Mind you there will be players with 0 in a certain Stat + a certain Skill, but if you're rolling like that, what are you doing lol.
Dice Pool (B):  +6 - +8         |   2, 3, 4 (successes on 5,6) as DC's for easy, medium hard. At this point, the whole curve grows fairly well. It's only dangerous to go below +5... so it would definitely not work for Stat + Skill = number of dice you throw
Dice Pool (C):  +6 - +8         |   You get success on 6 and the DC stays 1. At this point, the whole curve grows fairly well. It's only dangerous to go below +5... so it would definitely not work for Stat + Skill = number of dice you throw
